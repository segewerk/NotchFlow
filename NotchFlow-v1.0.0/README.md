# NotchFlow

**Your MacBook's notch, finally useful.** Meetings, mail, calendar, tasks and more – move your mouse to the top of the screen and it's all there.
**Dein Notch. Endlich nützlich.** Meetings, Mails, Kalender, Aufgaben und mehr – Maus nach oben, und alles ist da.

Free · macOS 14 Sonoma or later · Apple silicon & Intel · English & German · works on Macs with and without a notch

## Download

👉 **[Download NotchFlow-1.0.0.dmg](../../releases/latest)** (Releases on the right)

## Install (1 minute)

1. Open `NotchFlow-1.0.0.dmg` and drag **NotchFlow** into **Applications**.
2. The first time: **right-click NotchFlow → Open → Open**.
   On newer macOS: *System Settings → Privacy & Security → "Open Anyway"*.
   (NotchFlow isn't notarized by Apple yet – this is only needed once.)
3. A short welcome sets everything up. Then move your mouse to the notch – or press **⌥ Space**.

## Installieren (1 Minute)

1. `NotchFlow-1.0.0.dmg` öffnen und **NotchFlow** in **Programme** ziehen.
2. Beim ersten Mal: **Rechtsklick auf NotchFlow → Öffnen → Öffnen**.
   Auf neuerem macOS: *Systemeinstellungen → Datenschutz & Sicherheit → „Trotzdem öffnen“*.
3. Ein kurzes Willkommen richtet alles ein. Danach Maus nach oben an den Notch – oder **⌥ Leertaste**.

## Features

- **Meetings in one click** – Teams, Zoom, Meet and Webex links are detected; a notice appears before each meeting
- **All your mail** – Apple Mail (Outlook, Microsoft 365, Gmail, iCloud …) or any IMAP account; important senders first
- **Every calendar** – iCloud, Google, Outlook and calendar links, with a week view
- **For students** – Moodle, Canvas or Sakai: assignments, announcements, Word notes per course
- **Search anywhere (⌥ Space)** – apps, tasks with dates ("call mom fri"), unit & currency converter, timers
- **Clipboard history, file shelf, window layouts, focus timer, teleprompter, camera mirror, stocks, weather**
- **For developers** – approve Claude Code requests right in the notch, projects with Git status, AI usage limits
- **Profiles** – Minimal, Office, Students, Developers or Everything: only what you need is turned on

## Privacy

No account, no cloud, no tracking. Everything stays on your Mac. Passwords are stored locally, readable only by your user account. Mail is only ever read, never sent.

## Uninstall

Quit NotchFlow and move it to the Trash. Settings live in `~/Library/Application Support/NotchFlow`.

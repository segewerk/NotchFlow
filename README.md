NotchFlow

Your MacBook’s notch, finally useful.
Meetings, mail, calendar, tasks, clipboard history, developer tools, and more — all available right from the top of your screen.
Move your mouse to the notch or press ⌥ Space to open NotchFlow.
Free · macOS 14 Sonoma or later · Apple silicon & Intel · English & German · Works on Macs with and without a notch
Download
👉 Download NotchFlow-1.0.0.dmg from the Releases section
Installation
1. Download and open NotchFlow-1.0.0.dmg.
2. Drag NotchFlow into your Applications folder.
3. On first launch, right-click NotchFlow → Open → Open.
4. If macOS blocks the app, go to:
   System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway
NotchFlow is not yet notarized by Apple, so this step is only required once.

A short onboarding process will help you configure everything.
After that, simply move your mouse to the notch or press ⌥ Space.
Features
Meetings
See upcoming meetings directly in the notch.
NotchFlow automatically detects links for:
- Microsoft Teams
- Zoom
- Google Meet
- Webex
You can also receive a notification shortly before a meeting starts.
Mail
View your mail without opening your inbox.
Works with:
- Apple Mail
- Outlook
- Microsoft 365
- Gmail
- iCloud Mail
- IMAP accounts
Important senders can be prioritized so the messages that matter most appear first.
Calendar
See all your calendars in one place, including:
- iCloud
- Google Calendar
- Outlook
- Shared calendar links
Includes a simple weekly overview.
Student Tools
Connect supported learning platforms such as:
- Moodle
- Canvas
- Sakai
View assignments and announcements, and keep notes for individual courses.
Universal Search — ⌥ Space
Quickly search and perform actions from anywhere.
Examples:
- Launch apps
- Search tasks
- Create tasks using natural language
  call mom fri
- Convert units
- Convert currencies
- Start timers
Productivity Tools
NotchFlow also includes:
- Clipboard history
- File shelf
- Window layouts
- Focus timer
- Teleprompter
- Camera mirror
- Stocks
- Weather
Developer Tools
Built-in tools for developers include:
- Approve Claude Code requests directly from the notch
- View projects and Git status
- Monitor AI usage limits
- Quickly access development workflows
Profiles
Choose a profile depending on how you use your Mac:
- Minimal
- Office
- Student
- Developer
- Everything
Only the features you actually need are enabled.
Privacy
NotchFlow is designed to stay local.
- No account
- No cloud
- No tracking
- No analytics
- Your data stays on your Mac
Passwords are stored locally and are only accessible by your macOS user account.
Mail access is read-only. NotchFlow does not send emails.
Uninstall
To uninstall NotchFlow:
1. Quit NotchFlow.
2. Move the app from Applications to the Trash.
Settings and local data are stored in:
~/Library/Application Support/NotchFlow

You can delete this folder manually if you want to remove all NotchFlow data.
